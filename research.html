Research

I work on quantum computing in Fred Chong’s EPiQC lab at UChicago. In quantum optimal control, I think about which initializations get you better solutions for control pulses. I also work on quantum algorithms, from a theory project on Decoded Quantum Interferometry to running optimization experiments on IBM hardware.

EPiQC · 2024–present
Paper being finalized

Initialization in quantum optimal control

Gate-synthesis optimizers have to start from some initial guess, and the usual choice is random. We study initializations built from the geometry of the problem itself, using Euler–Arnold homotopy warm starts. On problems where every scheme converges, these starts reach fundamentally better solutions, so the starting point decides which solution you get.

My favorite part has been designing the experiments and watching the results change what we thought the paper was about.

Working toward a paper

Connecting Decoded Quantum Interferometry and the quantum harmonic oscillator

With two PhD collaborators, generalizing the Decoded Quantum Interferometry (DQI) algorithm to higher dimensions through an analogy with the quantum harmonic oscillator, to learn which problems higher-dimensional DQI can reach.

Q4bio
Paper in review

Quantum algorithms for dense biological data

A collaboration between our group, a UChicago cancer biology lab, and IBM, using QAOA to select features in biological data. I reworked our pipeline to sample from QAOA and RQAOA runs on IBM hardware, with low-energy post-selection.

MidIR Photonics Lab, UT Austin
Research assistant · summer 2024

Fabrication and laser experiments in mid-infrared photonics

In Dan Wasserman’s lab, I revived an abandoned vacuum system and developed and documented a metal-deposition protocol that opened a new fabrication setup for the group. I also automated ultrafast laser experiments to improve data quality and throughput.

PULSE-A CubeSat
Small Satellite Conference 2025

A polarization-based optical communications ground station

PULSE-A is UChicago’s student-led CubeSat mission to demonstrate a laser downlink from orbit using circular polarization. On the ground station team, I worked on the optical modeling in Zemax and designed the Reed–Solomon error-correction pipeline for encoding and decoding data.
